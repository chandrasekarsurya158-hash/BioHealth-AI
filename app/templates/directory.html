When to Seek Specialist Referral

Certified Dermatologist

Consult when skin lesions exhibit ABCDE criteria:

  • Asymmetrical border with irregular pigmentation
  • Diameter $> 6\text{ mm}$ or evolving rapidly over weeks
  • Spontaneous bleeding, ulceration, or chronic crusting
Pulmonologist / Chest Physician

Consult when thoracic symptoms include:

  • Persistent cough lasting $> 3\text{ weeks}$
  • Hemoptysis (coughing up blood), even in minor traces
  • Unexplained pleuritic chest pain or resting dyspnea
