Academic & Research Documentation

Multi-Domain AI Disease Detection & Intelligent Health Assistance

A unified, full-stack platform integrating specialized Artificial Intelligence architectures for plant pathology, staple crop protection, dermatological screening, and medical health assistance.

Project Objectives

1. Agricultural Security

Provide rapid identification of plant and crop leaf diseases (focusing on Rice, Wheat, and Maize) to prevent crop yield losses and recommend sustainable prevention practices.

2. Clinical Health Assistance

Provide early non-diagnostic health screening via skin lesions, chest X-rays, structured symptom triage, automated blood test report parsing, and verified medicine references.

Modular Architecture Overview

Module Domain AI / Technical Methodology Primary Output
Module 1: Plant Disease Agriculture Convolutional Neural Networks / Transfer Learning Disease class, confidence, prevention methods
Module 2: Crop Disease Agriculture Dedicated Staple Crop Classifier (Rice, Wheat, Maize) Pathogen symptoms, treatment guidance
Module 3: Skin Screening Healthcare Dermatology Research Classifier Visible pattern categorization + physician advisory

Module 4B: Blood Report Healthcare PyMuPDF Text Extraction + Regular Expressions Biomarker reference ranges (Hemoglobin, Glucose, etc.)

Module 4E: Medicine Info Healthcare Verified Pharmaceutical Knowledge Base Uses, contraindications, side effects
Ethical AI & Clinical Disclaimer

This software is built for educational, research, and non-critical preliminary screening purposes. It is strictly engineered to avoid claiming confirmed diagnostic capability. All medical assessments mandate formal review by certified clinicians, and agricultural recommendations must be cross-checked with certified agronomists.
